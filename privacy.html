Privacy and contact

Paws & Nines is sudoku with a cat who lives her own life. Česky

The game collects nothing about you. No account, no sign-up, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking. Nothing is sent to our server, because we do not have one.

What is stored, and where

Everything the game knows stays on your device, in the browser's own storage: the game you are playing, your saved games, the settings, the diary, the cat and the patterns of your handwriting if you write the numbers by hand. None of it goes anywhere and we cannot reach it. You can delete it at any time, in the game's settings or by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Backups

In the settings you can download a saved game as a file and load it again later. That file is yours, it sits wherever you put it, and it is never uploaded.

The internet

The game downloads itself (the page, the puzzles, the pictures, the sounds) and works without a connection from then on. It fetches nothing else and reports to nobody. Even the weather comes from inside: the sky is worked out on your device from the date and from the town you pick.

Children

The game is for a general audience. It collects no data at all, so it collects no data about children either.

Contact

A question, a bug, an idea or a privacy request: edc@edcbrno.com. We answer as fast as we can.

Changes

If any of this ever changes, it will say so here and in the game. Last updated: 23 September 2026.
